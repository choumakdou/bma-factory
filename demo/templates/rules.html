What a rule is

A rule says: a block of a sheet with these column names is this kind of section. The app tries your rules first, in the order you put them here. Anything a rule does not claim falls back to the built-in detection.

The four kinds are what the content page knows how to draw:

{% for k in kinds %} {{ k.label }} {% endfor %}

A rule matches on any of: every one of the columns in Has all of these columns; at least one of the columns in Has one of these columns; or a row where Column equals Value. Leave the column boxes empty to skip that test.
